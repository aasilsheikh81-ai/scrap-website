## Keyword gaps (competitors rank, we don't yet)
1. **"car scrap dealer in {city}"**. Competitors use "dealer"; we use "buyer". Add "scrap car dealer" as a secondary phrase in location-page copy and H2s.
2. **District and city modifiers:** Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Noida, Gurugram, Dehradun and others. Covered only as chips on our region pages. Next step: city pages or blog posts (plan #41–50).
3. **"govt authorised car scrapping" / "RVSF near me".** We can't target these honestly unless AutoScrape Pro is an RVSF or has a documented RVSF partner. **This is the single biggest gap.**
4. **"scrap car price" / "{model} scrap value".** Competitors have calculators. Build a simple weight-based estimator (plan #21–25).
5. **"10 year old diesel car Delhi".** A high-volume informational query that competitors capture with blog posts (plan #47).

## Content gaps
- **Price estimator tool:** competitors convert with one. Suggestion: model dropdown → kerb weight → range, with a disclaimer.
- **Proof content:** real yard photos (ours are stock), customer handover photos, Google reviews embedded, and the CoD sample (if an RVSF).
- **Process transparency:** a video of pickup to depollution to crushing.
- **Authorship / E-E-A-T:** an About page with the owner's name, years in business and registrations (GST, RVSF/ELV authorisation if held).

## Backlink opportunities
1. **Google Business Profile.** Create or verify it at the Ambala Road address, category "Scrap car buyer" or "Auto wrecker", and add service-area regions. *This drives the Saharanpur local pack more than anything on-site.*
2. **Citations with consistent NAP:** Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and the Saharanpur business directories.
3. **Government and industry lists:** if registered as or partnered with an RVSF, get listed on the Vahan/Parivahan RVSF directory, SIAM's list and state transport pages.
4. **Local press:** Amar Ujala and Dainik Jagran Saharanpur editions, and Tribune Punjab. Story angles: "vehicle scrapping explained", "X cars recycled this year".
5. **Partnerships:** car dealerships (exchange programs), insurance surveyors (total-loss vehicles), workshops, and RWAs in Noida/Gurugram. Each is a link plus a referral source.
6. **Guest posts** on auto blogs (CarToq, Team-BHP threads, answered carefully without spam) on "how to scrap a car" topics.
