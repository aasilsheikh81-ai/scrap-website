### Cadence
- **Weeks 1–4:** #1, 11, 12, 21, 22, 31, 47 (high volume, feed existing pages)
- **Weeks 5–8:** #2, 3, 7, 13, 28, 32, 41, 45
- **Weeks 9–12:** remaining city guides (#42–50) and cluster C model posts
- Then 2 posts per week.

### Writing standards
- 1,200–2,000 words for pillar posts (#1, 11, 21, 31, 47) and 800–1,200 for the rest.
- One H1, question-style H2s where they fit (featured snippets), and a 40–60 word direct answer under each H2.
- Include a short FAQ block with FAQPage schema, an author box (E-E-A-T), and an updated date.
- Never publish prices or incentive percentages without a source and a "last checked" date.
